# Yatra.AI — Interactive Prototype & Demo

> Experience the proposed end-to-end journey of Yatra.AI through an interactive prototype and product demonstration.

Yatra.AI is a multi-agent AI travel planning product designed to simplify the fragmented group travel experience — from trip discovery and collaborative planning to budgeting, booking, in-trip assistance, and post-trip engagement.

This folder contains the **interactive high-fidelity prototype** and **product demonstration recording** created to bring the product concept and user journey to life.

---

## 🚀 Interactive Prototype

Explore the end-to-end Yatra.AI experience:

🔗 https://euphonious-sherbet-2b8637.netlify.app/

The prototype demonstrates key product flows including:

- Trip creation
- Collaborative preference collection
- AI-assisted group consensus
- Destination discovery and review
- AI-generated itinerary
- Budget review
- UPI-based trip pooling
- Human approval before booking
- Booking confirmation
- Live travel assistance
- Post-trip expense settlement
- AI-powered trip memories and future recommendations
