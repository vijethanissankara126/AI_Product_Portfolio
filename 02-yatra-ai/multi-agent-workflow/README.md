# Yatra.AI — Multi-Agent Workflow

> A coordinated multi-agent system designed to simplify the end-to-end group travel journey.

Yatra.AI uses a **multi-agent AI architecture** to coordinate research, group decision-making, budget optimization, booking, in-trip assistance, and post-trip personalization.

Instead of relying on a single AI agent to perform every task, the system distributes responsibilities across **specialized agents**, coordinated through a shared orchestration layer.

---

## 🧭 Product Problem

Group travel planning is fragmented across conversations, travel platforms, spreadsheets, payment applications, and multiple information sources.

A typical journey involves:

```text
Research
   ↓
Compare Options
   ↓
Collect Group Preferences
   ↓
Reach Consensus
   ↓
Build Itinerary
   ↓
Check Budget
   ↓
Make Payments
   ↓
Book
   ↓
Manage Changes
   ↓
Handle Travel Disruptions
   ↓
Settle Expenses
